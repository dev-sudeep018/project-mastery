# Give a Sequence Position Signals

Every position receives a vector of oscillations at several frequencies. Fast channels distinguish nearby positions while slow channels change over a wider span.

Each sine/cosine pair uses the same angle. At position zero the sine channels are zero and cosine channels are one, providing a useful convention check.

Plot one fast pair and one slow pair over a longer sequence. Explain why repeating one frequency across all channels would provide less varied positional structure.
