# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to learners of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique work without attacking people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and spam are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect contributors. Decisions should be proportionate and explained privately when appropriate. Report ordinary conduct concerns to the repository owner through an available private GitHub contact channel; do not publish personal information in an issue. A dedicated reporting address is not yet established.
